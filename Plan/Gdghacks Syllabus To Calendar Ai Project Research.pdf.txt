SyllabusSync AI — Deep Research & Hackathon
Strategy
Core Idea
Build an AI-powered academic planning assistant that converts messy course syllabi, CourseLink
announcements, emails, and assignment descriptions into:
A unified academic calendar
Smart weekly task planning
AI-generated study breakdowns
Risk/overload detection
Auto-generated reminders and todos
Exportable calendar events (.ics / Google Calendar)
The goal is to solve a real student problem:
"I missed an assignment because the syllabus was 18 pages long and hidden inside random
PDFs and announcements."
This is strong hackathon material because:
Extremely relatable
Easy to demo visually
Uses AI meaningfully
Feels like a real startup product
Can be completed in 24 hours with MVP scope
Lets you show OCR, LLMs, structured extraction, and automation

Important Technical Challenge Research
Biggest Challenge: Date Extraction
Problem
Syllabi are inconsistent.
Examples:
Assignment 2 Due: October 11
vs
A2 submission deadline — 11 Oct.
vs
Week 5 deliverable (submit before midnight)
The parser must normalize all formats.•

•

Solution Strategy
Use LLMs for semantic extraction
Instead of regex-only parsing.
Workflow:
Extract raw text from PDF
Send chunks to Gemini
Ask for structured JSON
Validate schema
Store clean events
